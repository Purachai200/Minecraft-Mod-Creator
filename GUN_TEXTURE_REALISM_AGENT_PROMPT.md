# Agent Prompt: make the Youjo Senki Arms gun textures (and models) more realistic

## Goal
Improve the **realism of the gun textures and models** in the `Youjo Senki Arms` mod (a TACZ add-on for Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge).
Today the textures are procedural and flat: blued steel looks like grey plastic, the wood grain is too uniform, and parts have few details.
The guns should look like **real WWI-era firearms** in the blocky but detailed style of the built-in TACZ guns.

Work folder: `Mod Minecraft Creator/Youjo Senki Arms/`

## How the assets are made (read this first)
Every gun model and texture is **generated by a script**. Do not hand-edit the PNG or JSON files: they are overwritten on the next run.

- `tools/build_gun_assets.py`: the single source of truth
  - `box()`, `rod()` (octagonal rod), `slab()` (sloped box), `profile()` (bevelled wooden stock), `sling()`, `cartridge()`, `shotshell()`: geometry helpers
  - One builder function per gun: `mauser_rifle()` (Kar98k / Gewehr 98), `t_gewehr()`, `c96()`, `mp18()`, `luger()`, `mosin()`, `lebel()`, `lee_enfield()`, `mg08()` (MG 08/15 and MG 08), `m1897()`, `kampfpistole()`
  - `texel(m, face, i, j, ...)`: paints one texel by **material** (`wood`, `steel`, `dark`, `bright`, `brass`, `copper`, `leather`, `glow`, `olive`, `cloth`, `red`, `groove`)
  - `decal()`: per-face details (`crest`, `holes`, `ribs`, `panel`, `screw`, `serial`, `groove`), set on a box with `decal="..."`
  - `P = 12` texels per model unit; atlases are 512 or 1024 and grow automatically
  - Wood uses **world-space** coordinates (`world=`) so the grain flows across cube seams. Keep that.
- Run (from `Youjo Senki Arms/`): `python3 tools/build_gun_assets.py`. It writes the geo, texture, slot icon, HUD art and display for every gun into `src/main/resources/tacz_youjoarms/assets/youjoarms/`.
- Preview without starting the game: `python3 tools/preview_geo.py <geo.json> <texture.png> <out.png>` renders the right side, left side and two 3/4 views.

## Hard constraints (breaking these breaks the guns in game)
1. **Do not rename, remove or re-parent bones.** Each gun keeps the bone tree of a TACZ donor gun (m700, m1911, hk_mp5a5, m249, m870, m320) so TACZ's animations drive it. Only cubes and textures change.
2. **Keep the gameplay anchors where they are:**
   - Bore height and the sight line: front sight top and rear notch bottom must stay on the `iron_view` y (8.406 on m700 rifles, 6.175 on pistols, 11.0 on MGs, the overridden values on MP18 / M1897 / Kampfpistole). Aiming breaks otherwise.
   - Barrel length and the `_pivots` overrides (`muzzle_pos`, `muzzle_flash`): attachments such as suppressors mount there.
   - The grip and trigger area: the hands are animated to those positions.
   - Receiver top under `scope_pos`: attachment scopes sit there.
3. Moving parts stay in their bones: bolt in `rotate`, slide in `slide`, magazine in `mag_default` / `mag_standard` / `box`, cover in `cap`.
4. Coordinates: front of the gun is **-Z**, **right side is -X**, 1 unit = 1/16 block.
5. Keep the cyan Elenium rune band (`glow`) on Empire guns only. Enemy guns (Mosin, Lebel, Lee-Enfield) have none.
6. Do not touch the spell system (`src/main/java/.../formula`, `MagicCircleRenderer`) or the gun data balance.

## What to improve (in priority order)
1. **Steel:** real blued steel is blue-black with cool highlights along the top edges, a slight sheen, and wear to bare silver on high points: muzzle, sight edges, bolt handle, trigger-guard bottom. Add a subtle vertical gradient, speckle and edge wear driven by world position, not random noise per face.
2. **Wood:** walnut and beech with long flowing grain, darker end grain, oil sheen on top surfaces, darker handling marks at the wrist and fore-end grip, and small dents and scratches.
3. **Small details** using decals or small boxes: screw heads on bands and butt plates, cross-bolts in the stock, cleaning-rod tip, sling swivels, proof stamps and serials (already started in the `serial` decal), checkering on the C96 and Luger grips, and the MG 08 jacket's ribs and filler cap.
4. **Per-gun accuracy:** compare each builder against reference photos of the real gun (silhouette, part positions, proportions) and correct what is off. Examples: Kar98k cupped butt plate; Mosin hexagonal receiver; Lebel two-piece stock; Lee-Enfield nose cap; MG 08/15 feed block and stock shape.
5. Use more cubes where it pays off (rounder barrels and receivers, bevelled edges). Watch the texture atlas size: 1024 is the ceiling.
