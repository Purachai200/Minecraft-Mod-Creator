# Military Vehicle Realism & Component Taxonomy

This skill defines the mandatory hardware components, mechanical greebles, and detailing required to make military transport vehicles look authentic, heavy, and battle-ready rather than simplistic toys.

---

## 1. Front-End Hardware Taxonomy

1. **Tubular Steel Bullbars & Brush Guards:**
   - Multi-tier tubular construction with angled headlight hoops.
   - Heavy uprights bolted directly to the frame chassis rails.
   - Recessed round or rectangular LED fog lamps protected inside the bumper cutouts.
2. **Recovery Winch Assembly:**
   - Centered heavy spool with forged steel drum and cable coils.
   - 4-way roller fairlead (two horizontal rollers, two vertical rollers).
   - High-visibility safety orange forged clevis hook hooked to a bumper eye.
   - Dual forged D-ring recovery shackles on both bumper horns.
3. **Deep-Fording Air Snorkel:**
   - Follows the A-pillar contour at a $25^\circ-30^\circ$ rake.
   - Crowned with a cylindrical cyclone pre-cleaner canister or forward-facing ram scoop.
4. **Front Glacis & Hood Detailing:**
   - Raised power bulge or armor cowl.
   - Recessed cooling louvers with fine wire mesh underneath.
   - Heavy external hood latches and rubber bump stops for folding windshields.

---

## 2. Armored Cab & Crew Ergonomics

1. **Ballistic Windshield & Vision Blocks:**
   - Thick frame bevels with visible torx/hex bolt studs.
   - Anti-glare tinted ballistic glass (subtle cyan/green tint).
   - Heavy-duty pantograph wiper arms mounted at the cowl.
2. **Roof Equipment Array:**
   - Dual-row high-output LED off-road light bar in extruded aluminum housing.
   - Low-profile tactical roof rack with tie-down perimeter rails.
   - Heavy spring-mounted high-gain whip antenna on the rear cab pillar.
3. **Crew Cab Interior:**
   - High-back military bucket seats with integrated headrests and 5-point harness straps.
   - Detailed steering column with 3-spoke military wheel.
   - Tactical center console with communications radio displays and weapon clamp racks.

---

## 3. Rear Cargo Bed & Logistics Rigging

1. **Headache Rack / Roll-Cage:**
   - Heavy tubular steel roll bar directly behind the cab glass.
   - Backward-facing LED scene floodlights for illuminating the cargo bed at night.
   - Rearward diagonal kicker bars transferring loads down to the rear axle frame.
2. **Bed Deck & Tie-Down System:**
   - Heavy diamond-plate (tread-plate) steel bed floor.
   - Recessed D-ring tie-down anchor pockets spaced every 1.0 block along the deck perimeter.
   - Heavy orange ratchet straps with cast-steel ratchet tensioner buckles.
3. **Field Equipment & Stowage (Greebles):**
   - NATO steel Jerrycans mounted in lockable steel cage racks.
   - Waffle-pattern high-density polymer recovery traction boards mounted on bedside rails.
   - Entrenching shovel, pickaxe, and heavy axe strapped to bulkheads with leather buckles.
4. **Articulated Drop Tailgate:**
   - Heavy greaseable barrel hinges at the bottom deck lip.
   - T-handle latch mechanisms on both top corners.
   - Internal diamond-plate surface so it acts as a non-slip walk-on ramp when lowered $90^\circ$.

---

## 4. Chassis, Suspension & Running Gear

1. **Heavy Off-Road Running Gear:**
   - Deep rock/mud lug treads with alternating self-cleaning voids.
   - Steel beadlock wheel rings with a full circle of 8 to 12 perimeter locking bolts.
   - Heavy solid axle tubes with cast iron differential pumpkins.
   - External reservoir remote-canister coilover shock absorbers.
2. **Underbody Armor:**
   - Heavy stamped steel skid plates protecting engine oil pan, transmission, and transfer case.
   - Tubular rock slider bars running under door sills between front and rear wheel wells.
3. **Exhaust & Lighting:**
   - Tucked high-clearance exhaust tailpipe exiting ahead of rear tires or turned down behind axle.
   - Dual blackout military tail light clusters protected by steel wire stone cages.
   - Heavy rubber mudflaps hanging behind rear wheels with anti-sail brackets.
